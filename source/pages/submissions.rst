===============================
Call for Sessions and Abstracts
===============================

Geoinformatics Research Days welcomes contributions from all areas of geospatial research and applications,
providing an open platform for sharing ideas, methods, and innovations across the discipline.
We welcome contributions ranging from early-stage research and practical experiences to innovative methods and new ideas.

Call for Sessions
=================

We invite proposals for sessions. If you and your colleagues share a topic, question, or approach that deserves dedicated time,
you are welcome to organize a session around it. This could be a regular session featuring presentations, a special themed session,
a workshop, or possibly another format of your choice.

**A session proposal should include:**

- A descriptive session title
- The names, emails and affiliations of the organizers
- A description of the session and its relevance, maximum 300 words
- The preferred format (e.g. paper session, panel, workshop, or discussion)

The proposals will be reviewed by the organizing committee, and accepted sessions will be published on this website.

**Propose your session by 27 November 2026.**

Call for Abstracts
==================

Authors can submit an abstract to one of the accepted sessions or to a general topic. 
You’re also encouraged to contact the session organizers if you’re unsure if your topic is fit for the session or not. 

**Submit your abstract by 8 February 2027.**

Topics of Interest
------------------

Submissions are welcome in, but not limited to, the following areas:

1. Environment, Biodiversity, and Natural Resources
2. Climate, Hazards, and Resilience
3. Cities, Mobility, and Planning
4. Health, Wellbeing, and Spatial Justice
5. Earth Observation and Remote Sensing
6. Spatial Analysis, Statistics, and Modelling
7. GeoAI and Machine Learning
8. 3D, Digital Twins, and Positioning
9. Geovisualization, Cartography, and Storytelling
10. Open Data, Citizen Science, and Responsible Practices
11. Education, Tools, and Applications in the Public Sector and Industry
12. Other Topics in GIScience and Geoinformatics

Submission Guidelines
---------------------

Presenters are invited to submit an abstract of a maximum of 300 words.

All submitted abstracts will be peer-reviewed to ensure the quality of the contributions.
Accepted submissions will be selected for either an oral or poster presentation at the *Geoinformatics Research Days 2027*.

-------------------
